# ACERVO Brand Foundation

## Brand

### Confirmed

- **ACERVO** is the brand and product-family name.
- The brand represents accumulated, structured, and connected knowledge.

## Product Family

### Confirmed

- **ACERVO DOC** is the first product in the ACERVO family.

## Visual Identity

### Confirmed

- The visual language is geometric and draws on nodes and connections.

### Still to be defined

- The specific geometry and the remaining visual-identity guidelines.

## Color System

### Confirmed

- `#1A1818` — ACERVO primary dark/background.
- `#1F375B` — ACERVO primary blue / family identity.
- `#A5D47C` — ACERVO green / family identity.
- `#D8C4A0` — ACERVO DOC document/folder color.
- `#F5EF42` — ACERVO DOC highlight/accent.

ACERVO blue and green form the core family identity. Product-specific colors belong to individual products and should not replace the ACERVO family identity. Colors may be used in different tonal variations to create depth, but the palette should remain visually coherent rather than treating every color as an equal-weight element.
